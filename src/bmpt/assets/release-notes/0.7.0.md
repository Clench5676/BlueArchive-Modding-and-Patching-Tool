## What's New

- **Student Hall:** select a portrait by its visible outline; each portrait gets its own toolbar, its own size and a quick skin swap
- **Top bar and bottom menu:** they come in as the game's lobby brings them in; in fullscreen and the camera they stay out of sight until the pointer reaches the window's top or bottom edge
- **Memorial lobby dialogue** in the game's own speech balloon
- Popups, buttons, chips and info boxes follow the game's own design throughout the App

## Changes

- Student Hall: portraits stay fixed to the scene while panning; the portrait and background pickers use the game's popup footer
- Batch Import: the list, name plates, filter and Import button match the game's popups
- Includes BA Modding Toolkit v2.12.0

## Fixes

- Files from a mod always stay inside their output folder
- Safer Mod library: a damaged state or settings file no longer loses your library, a failed library move can be retried, and each Mod updates as a whole
- macOS: the top bar moves the window again after a popup or fullscreen, the window buttons stay on wide or second displays, and the game's pointer no longer flickers to the system pointer
- Camera: blank space in the Expressions list no longer pans the camera
- Clearer error messages, keyboard focus kept inside popups, and many text fit and layout details

## 新功能

- **学生大厅：** 按立绘的可见轮廓选中；每个立绘有自己的工具栏，可单独调整大小、快速换皮
- **顶栏和底栏：** 按游戏大厅的方式进场；全屏和拍摄时隐藏，指针移到窗口上下边缘时伸出
- **记忆大厅台词** 使用游戏自己的对话气泡
- 弹窗、按钮、标签和信息框全面采用游戏自身的设计

## 变更

- 学生大厅：平移时立绘固定在场景上；立绘和背景选择改用游戏弹窗的底部按钮区
- 批量导入：列表、名牌、筛选和导入按钮与游戏弹窗一致
- 包含 BA Modding Toolkit v2.12.0

## 修复

- Mod 中的文件始终留在输出文件夹内
- Mod 库更安全：状态或设置文件损坏不再丢失 Mod 库，Mod 库迁移失败可以重试，每个 Mod 整体更新
- macOS：弹窗或全屏后顶栏可再次拖动窗口；宽屏或第二块屏幕上窗口按钮不再消失；游戏指针不再闪成系统指针
- 拍摄：表情列表的空白处不再平移镜头
- 错误提示更清楚，弹窗内保持键盘焦点，修复多处文字适配和布局细节
